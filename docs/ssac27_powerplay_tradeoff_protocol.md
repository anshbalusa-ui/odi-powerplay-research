## Question and estimand

For an eligible men's ODI team-innings row, estimate batting-team eventual win probability conditional on first-ten-overs runs `R`, wickets lost `W`, and documented pre-match/match context `C`. The primary contrast is the *additional* PP runs `d >= 0` satisfying `p(R+d, W+1, C) = p(R, W, C)` for `W=1` at development-median `R`; solve by bracketed numerical predictions within development-only observed support. If no nonnegative supported root exists, report `undefined` with a reason; never invent an exchange rate or describe deliberate wicket loss as beneficial. Report modeled win-probability differences for fixed-run and fixed-wicket comparisons alongside run-equivalent values and their match-cluster confidence intervals. These are predictive/associational decision benchmarks, not intervention effects or an optimal strategy.

## Model, context and support

Fit on development only. Use one interpretable L2-penalized logistic regression with the prespecified current `C=1`, `liblinear`, development-fitted numeric imputation/scaling and categorical encoding; no 2024 preprocessing fit. Predictors are powerplay runs and wickets, batting order, pre-match Elo difference and earlier-date team experience/history, toss, competition, year/rule-era, and strictly prior-date venue scoring history; no pitch field or outcome/future variable is a predictor. Venue history is a prior scoring environment, **not** a measurement of the focal prepared pitch. Keep both team innings per match in the same split and match-cluster resample; because their binary winners are complementary, never treat 1,884 unlocked innings as independent matches or fit match fixed effects when the target is calibrated absolute win probability.

The context-dependent primary model has six predeclared PP interactions: `R` and `W` each with batting-first, pre-match Elo difference, and the *same* earlier-date mean PP runs at that venue. Fit the additive logistic benchmark and four-term innings/Elo interaction sensitivity without choosing a winner on validation results. If development rank/support invalidates the six-term specification, report the relevant estimands undefined rather than silently selecting an attractive simpler model. Prespecify first/chase × Elo difference (development median minus one development SD, median, plus one SD) × prior-venue mean PP runs (development available-history P25, median, P75). Hold nuisance covariates at recorded development modal/median values. Starting `R` is the overall development median rounded half up; primary `W:1→2`, sensitivity `0→1` and `2→3`. Develop-only local support: exact innings and wicket state, Elo/venue within one development SD of the context, at least 20 distinct matches per state, and a wicket-state-specific 5th–95th run envelope. Nuisance category levels must exist in development but do not subdivide support neighborhoods. Report all up-to-18 primary cells, their support status and undefined reasons, not a selectively chosen strongest contrast. The numerical root is nonnegative, uses a fixed support bracket and 0.01-run bisection tolerance, and cannot extrapolate beyond support.
